# Loading背景画像

- 生成方法：内蔵image_genツール
- 保存先：Data/Sprite/LoadingBackground.png
- 用途：Loading／ステージ開始画面の背景。文字は後からTextRendererで重ねる想定。
- 参考：[ロックマンX4のステージ開始画面が掲載されたページ](https://yu-face.hatenablog.com/entry/2018/08/27/045653)
- 今回は画像の作成・保存のみ。SceneLoadingへの組み込みはまだ行っていません。

## 使用したプロンプト

Create one finished full-screen background image asset for a Japanese 2D action game's Loading / stage-introduction screen. Homage to the metallic silver stage/boss introduction backgrounds of Rockman X4 (Mega Man X4), NOT the stage selection map. Landscape 16:9, ideally 1280 x 720 pixels. Flat front-facing 2D game background, late-1990s PlayStation sci-fi aesthetic: broad horizontal bands of brushed silver, cool steel gray and charcoal with clean metallic gradients, restrained icy blue highlights, dark graphite top and bottom framing strips, subtle horizontal scanline texture and slim mechanical bevels. A large uncluttered horizontal silver-gray central field occupies most of the image, with enough quiet negative space across the middle for stage-name and description text that the game will draw later; the right-center also remains clear for a future character sprite. Strong horizontal composition, readable and restrained, crisp graphical edges, slight retro pixel-art sensibility, not a photorealistic room, not a modern hologram dashboard. Very subtle angular panel cuts near the outer edges only. The central field should be medium-light silver, avoiding pure white glare, detailed circuitry, or high-frequency clutter behind text. Top and bottom dark bands provide contrasting areas for later headings and a loading label. Edge-to-edge complete background, no mockup device, no outer margins. Absolutely no letters, words, numbers, logos, symbols resembling text, characters, portraits, silhouettes, progress bars, loading icons, watermarks, or existing game screenshots. Opaque background. Deliver the background image alone.
